BSc (Hons) thesis · 2026

Ray tracing and equivalent geodesic lenses for the Morse lens

X. Zheng — Xiamen University Malaysia, Sepang, Malaysia

We construct the equivalent geodesic-lens representation of the Morse refractive-index profile, show that the conformal Morse lens is the (A = B, C = 1) subclass of Cornbleet's general family, and validate the construction by ray tracing.

About关于

The short version, in two languages.

I graduated from Xiamen University Malaysia with a BSc (Hons) in physics; since autumn 2026 I have been pursuing an MSc at Western University in London, Ontario. I care about the relationship between geometry and light: a ray travelling through a curved space follows a geodesic, so designing the metric means designing the path.

我在厦门大学马来西亚分校取得物理学荣誉学士学位，2026 年秋起在 Western University 攻读硕士。 我关心几何与光的关系：射线在弯曲空间里沿测地线走，设计度规就等于设计光路。
